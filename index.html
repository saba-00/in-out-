About India Skills

Elevating excellence in skill assessment for over a decade

A knowledge-management company built on one idea: assessment results should be clear, unbiased, and something both candidates and organisations can act on with confidence.

India Skills Pvt. Ltd. has spent 14 years building a reputation for clear, unbiased assessments. As an NCVET-approved agency, we're empanelled across 18 sectors and work closely with government, semi-government and industry bodies on PAN-India skill development initiatives.

Our in-house technology platform, TAGSCORE, powers seamless online and blended assessments at scale — around 20,000 assessments every month, with 12 lakh+ candidates assessed across 72 sectors, backed by a content library of 35,000+ question papers.

Every assessment is conducted by expert assessors drawn from premier institutes, so results hold up wherever they're presented — a government audit, an employer's hiring decision, or a training institute's own quality benchmark.

We also partner directly with private training institutes: their learners are assessed against national standards through us, giving those institutes a credible, third-party certification route.

Our Mission

To be the preferred skill assessment organisation for Government, Industry and NGOs — through best practices, an exceptional team and a focus on excellence, with trust, integrity and accountability driving every assessment.

Our Vision

To raise the standard of vocational training delivery by ensuring high-quality assessment and certification, closing the gap between the demand for skilled manpower and its supply.
